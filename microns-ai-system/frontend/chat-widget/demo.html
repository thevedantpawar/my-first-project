Microns chat widget

This page exists to test the embed. The bubble in the bottom-right talks to POST /leads/chat on the backend and walks a visitor through the six qualification questions.

Embed snippet

<script
  src="https://api.your-clinic.com/widget/microns-chat.js"
  data-api="https://api.your-clinic.com"
  data-clinic="Radiance Med Spa"
  data-color="#0f766e"
  data-title="Chat with us"
  data-position="right"
  defer></script>

Attributes

  • data-api — backend base URL. Defaults to the page's origin.
  • data-clinic — name shown in the header.
  • data-color — brand colour for the bubble and buttons.
  • data-title — launcher label.
  • data-position — right (default) or left.

Opening it from your own button

<button onclick="MicronsChat.open()">Book a consultation</button>

Before going live

  • Add your site's origin to CORS_ORIGINS in .env.
  • Serve the backend over HTTPS — browsers block mixed content.
  • Put a rate limiter in front of /leads/chat; the built-in one is per-worker.
