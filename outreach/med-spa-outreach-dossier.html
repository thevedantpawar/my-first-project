The five automations, as built

01
No-show prevention
Hourly cron, 24h + 2h reminders. Idempotent — a cron that fires twice still texts once.
02
Reactivation
Flags no-shows and 45-day dormant patients, texts a booking link, then a credit offer at day 3. 30-day cooldown.
03
Review request
Fires on treatment-completed, AI-drafts replies for manager approval. Public auto-post ships disabled.
04
Lead qualification
Chat + SMS, six questions, scored 0–100, routed hot / warm / cold. Pregnancy and blood-thinner gates sit outside the arithmetic.
05
Voice handoff
24/7 phone agent that never answers a clinical question — routes to a provider callback on a tracked 2-hour SLA.

The eleven, in priority order

How these emails are built

Written to the cold-email spec, so they may look under-sold next to a normal pitch. Subjects are two to four words, lowercase, and deliberately boring — the subject's only job is the open. Each email opens on their world rather than yours, carries one proof point instead of a feature list, and closes on an interest-based ask (“worth a look?”) rather than a request for thirty minutes. All land between 70 and 110 words. Remove the opening line from any of them and the rest stops making sense — that's the test that the personalization is load-bearing rather than decorative.
