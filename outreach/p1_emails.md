# P1 first-touch emails — 11 leads

Written to the cold-email spec: 2–4 word lowercase subject, opens on their world,
one proof point, interest-based close, 70–110 words. Sender sign-off omitted — add yours.

Where a name is unknown the email opens on the observation, not a greeting. Do not
write "Hi there" — no greeting reads better than a wrong one.
