MICRONS (“we”, “us”) provides AI automation services to medical spas in the United States. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a Revenue Leak Audit you submit your name, business name, location, email, phone number, approximate lead volume and the operational gap you want to discuss. We also collect standard server logs and, if enabled, privacy-respecting analytics.

How we use it

To contact you about the audit you requested, to prepare for that call, and to provide services if you become a client. We do not sell your information.

Scheduling

If you book a call, scheduling is handled by Calendly, which processes the name, email and any details you provide in order to create the event. See Calendly’s own privacy policy for how they handle that data.

Client data

When we build systems for a client, patient and customer data is processed only to operate the automations that client has approved, under a separate services agreement and, where applicable, a HIPAA business associate agreement.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@microns.ai.
