# Outreach Assets — ready to send

**Rules that matter more than the copy:**
- Send from a real domain, not Gmail, if you have one. If you only have Gmail, send fewer and make them count.
- One link maximum. No attachments, no images, no HTML.
- Send Tue–Thu, 7–9am local time to the recipient.
- **UAE timing:** Mon–Wed mornings Gulf time. Friday is dead — prayers and the short week. Never send Friday afternoon.
- Every email below ends on a **genuine question**, not a meeting request. That's deliberate: questions get replies, calendar links get ignored.
- Personalise nothing further. These are already researched — adding generic flattery ("love what you're building") actively weakens them.

# WHAT NOT TO DO

- **Don't send all 20 on day one.** Send Tier 1, learn from the replies, adjust, then send Tier 2. Burning the whole list on an untested message is the most common way this fails.
- **Don't attach anything.** Attachments trip spam filters and nobody opens them cold.
- **Don't use a calendar link in the first email.** It shifts work onto them and signals volume outreach.
- **Don't follow up more than three times.** Four is where you become the person they screenshot.
- **Don't say "AI automation."** Every one of these founders gets ten of those a week. Say what breaks and what it costs.
