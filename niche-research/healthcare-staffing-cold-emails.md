# Cold Email Scripts: Healthcare Staffing Agencies

Target: US healthcare staffing agencies (travel nursing, per diem / local, allied health) with 5–100 recruiters.
Positioning: **"I build AI applicant response and credentialing systems for healthcare staffing agencies."**

Placeholders in `{{double_braces}}` are filled per prospect. Text in `[square brackets]` is yours to replace.

---

## Why the offer changes for healthcare

The general recruitment scripts lead with **outbound sourcing** (finding candidates). Healthcare staffing is different:

- **Their problem is speed, not finding people.** Agencies spend heavily on job ads (Indeed, Vivian Health, their own site), and nurses apply to several agencies at once. The recruiter who calls first usually gets the nurse.
- **Credentialing slows starts.** A missing BLS card, TB test, reference or skills checklist can push a start date back, and the facility may give the job to another agency.
- **Nurses leave at contract end.** Travel contracts usually run about 13 weeks. If nobody offers a traveler the next assignment 3–4 weeks before the end date, another agency will.
- **The database goes unused.** They've paid to get every nurse in their ATS through ads, yet past travelers rarely get contacted again.
- **Margins are tighter than at the 2021–22 peak,** so doing more with the same recruiters matters.

### What you're selling (4 modules, lead with #1)

1. **Instant applicant response.** Every applicant gets a text and email within about 2 minutes, day or night. It checks licence state (or a multistate compact licence), specialty, years of experience, preferred locations, start date and shift preference. It then books qualified nurses onto a recruiter's calendar and logs everything in the ATS (Bullhorn, LaborEdge, Ceipal or whatever they use).
2. **Credential and document chasing.** A checklist for each facility; automatic text and email reminders for missing documents; AI reads each upload to check document type and expiry date; alerts for licences and certifications expiring within 60 days.
3. **Redeployment and extensions.** The system watches contract end dates. Three or four weeks out, it sends each traveler a shortlist of matching open jobs and alerts their recruiter.
4. **Database reactivation and job matching.** When a new job order comes in, it matches nurses already in the database (by specialty, licence state and availability) and texts or emails the best fits the same day.

**Pricing (US, estimate):** setup $4,000–$8,000, retainer $2,000–$3,000/mo. Paid pilot on Module 1 only: $1,500–$2,500 for 3 weeks.

**Your free proof (you don't have case studies yet):** a **custom demo on their own job post**. Build the applicant-response flow for one of their live jobs. Record a 3-minute video of a test applicant applying, getting screened by text and landing on the calendar. Send the video; don't pitch.

> Don't "mystery shop" them by submitting fake applications. It wastes their recruiters' time and starts the relationship with a trick.

## Who to email and where to find them

- **Titles:** Owner, President, CEO, VP of Recruitment, Director of Recruiting, Director of Operations. At larger agencies, also the Director of Credentialing or Compliance for the credentialing angle.
- **Agency size:** 5–100 recruiters. Skip the very largest national firms, which build in-house.
- **Signals for personalization:** active travel or per diem job ads (their website, Indeed, Vivian Health), job posts for recruiters or credentialing specialists, a new state or specialty expansion.
- **Where to find them:** Apollo or Sales Navigator ("Staffing and Recruiting" plus keywords like travel nurse, healthcare staffing, per diem, allied), Vivian Health agency listings, and staffing association member directories.

## Healthcare-specific guardrails (mention them on calls; it builds trust)

- **Texting consent (TCPA).** Only text applicants who have opted in. Add consent wording to their application form ("I agree to receive texts about job opportunities…"). Never cold-text nurses who didn't apply. Cold outreach to the **agency** stays email.
- **Sensitive documents.** Licences, immunization records and background checks are sensitive personal data. Store them only in the agency's own systems, limit access, and sign an NDA or data processing agreement if asked.
- **Credentialing standards.** Agencies with Joint Commission staffing certification are audited on credential tracking. Position Module 2 as supporting their existing process, with a human reviewing every file, not replacing it.
- **Human decisions.** AI screens for basic fit and schedules. Recruiters decide who gets submitted.
- **Email.** Follow CAN-SPAM: real sender, physical address, working opt-out. Send from a separate warmed-up domain, 30–50 emails per inbox per day, plain text, no links in Email 1.
